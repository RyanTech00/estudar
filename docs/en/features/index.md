# Overview

Estudar has four tabs — **Today, Week, Timer, Progress** — and three management screens: **the plan**, **the degree record** and **the server**. On a phone the navigation sits at the bottom; on a computer, in a sidebar.

## Features

| Feature | What it does | Principle |
|---|---|---|
| [AI weekly plan](/en/features/ai-plan) | Builds the week from your subjects, dates and hours; checks the result against fixed rules | Spacing, interleaving, retrieval |
| [Timer and focus mode](/en/features/timer-and-focus) | Full-screen 40+10 blocks; the block runs on real clock time and survives the app being closed | Blocks with breaks |
| [Block log](/en/features/block-log) | Attempts, confidence and help **before** marking; correct answers after | Attempt before revealing; calibration |
| [Closed-book tests and mastery](/en/features/closed-book-tests) | Mastery comes only from paper tests, with no notes, taken after a delay; assisted practice is kept separate | Unaided retrieval |
| [Degree record](/en/features/degree-record) | Modules by year and semester, assessments, exam periods, ECTS-weighted average, prerequisites, import from a photo | Exam deadlines; prior knowledge |
| [Semester report](/en/features/semester-report) | Average, grades and what your tests predicted | Measure before deciding |
| [Sync and offline](/en/features/sync) | Phone ↔ computer in real time; works without a connection | — |

## What's missing (on purpose)

- **AI-generated summaries** — summarising and rereading have low utility (Dunlosky et al., 2013). The app doesn't offer them as a way to study.
- **An AI tutor that gives answers** — it harms unaided performance (Bastani et al.). If there is ever a tutor, it will only step in after you have tried. See the [roadmap](/en/roadmap).
- **Leaderboards, points and badges for hours** — they would reward the wrong signal.
